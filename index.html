02 / Controls in production

Data protection by design

Privacy policy written against Ghana's Data Protection Act. Phone numbers, MoMo numbers and one-time codes are scrubbed from error logs.

Act 843 · 2012

Least-privilege access

Database access is denied by default and opened per table and column. Every rule is tested as a real user, including the cases that must be refused.

Audit trail

Every admin action is logged with a before-and-after record, and every product change is written up: problem, evidence, fix, verification.

Tested backups

Nightly encrypted off-platform backups, and a monthly drill that restores one into a clean database and checks every table.

KYC-informed onboarding

Phone verification at signup, and the MoMo account name must match the user's name, checked on the server.

Act 1044 · 2020

Consumer protection

Payments are held until delivery. If a seller doesn't confirm they still have the item, the order cancels itself with a full refund.

Customer funds are held by a licensed payment service provider, not by GrabAm, after evaluating the escrow licensing rules in Ghana's Payment Systems and Services Act, 2019 (Act 987).
